About Amanuel Production

Welcome to Amanuel Production, a professional photography and video service based in Manchester, UK.

We specialise in capturing important life moments including weddings, graduations, birthdays, baptisms, pre-wedding sessions and other special occasions.

Our mission is simple: to create beautiful memories that you can keep forever. We focus on natural emotions, creative photography and high-quality results.

Every event is unique, so we work closely with our clients to understand their ideas and provide a personalised photography experience.

Although we are based in Manchester, we are available for events across the UK and are happy to discuss travel opportunities for special projects.
